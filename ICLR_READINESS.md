Freeze correction: the encoder winner does **not** freeze its action-producing bidirectional language stack. In `full_duplicate` mode, `language_model.encoder_layers = copy.deepcopy(language_model.layers)`, so all encoder and causal-decoder transformer parameters are untied. With `separate_cross_attention:true`, decoder embeddings, decoder norm and LM head are also deep-copied, and decoder cross-attention uses separate adapters. The configured freeze list freezes that untied causal decoder, decoder embeddings/norm and LM head; `skip_decoder:true` omits it at action rollout. Thus “full VLM tuning versus frozen encoder language layers” is not a valid explanation for the score gap.
